Privacy Policy

Aqua Infinity (Android app eg.aquainfinity.app) · Effective 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Aqua Infinity app collects, why, and the choices you have. Aqua Infinity sells and services home water filters in Egypt.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and/or phone number, and your password (stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Delivery details — the addresses you save and, if you allow it, your device's precise location, used to fill in your delivery address and deliver your order.
  • Orders and payments — the products you order, order status, and the payment method you choose. For bank/wallet transfers (e.g. Vodafone Cash, InstaPay) you upload a photo of the transfer receipt so we can confirm payment. We do not collect card numbers.
  • Your filters and service — filters you register (by scanning their QR code or selecting a model), installation dates, usage information you enter, service requests, and loyalty points.
  • Photos and camera — the camera is used to scan product QR codes and, if you choose, to photograph a payment receipt. QR scans are not stored; receipt photos are stored privately with your order.
  • Notifications — a device push token, so we can send you order updates and filter reminders.
  • App settings — your language and display preferences, stored on your device.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders, and verify payments.
  • To track your filters' health and remind you when cartridges need changing.
  • To send notifications you can turn off at any time in your device settings.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage (data encrypted in transit).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Fonts — downloading the fonts the app displays.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete account) or by request — see Delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, orders, addresses, registered filters, notifications, loyalty points, service requests and uploaded receipts.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so each customer can only access their own information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.

  • الطلبات والدفع — المنتجات التي تطلبها وحالة الطلب وطريقة الدفع. عند الدفع بالتحويل (فودافون كاش، إنستاباي) ترفع صورة إيصال التحويل لتأكيد الدفع. لا نجمع بيانات البطاقات البنكية.
